Role health, how deep the pipeline is
Not healthy under 2 candidates past matching, and 3 or fewer fresh people left in matching, so there is nothing coming and nothing to send
Thin exactly 1 candidate past matching, with only 4 or 5 fresh people behind them, so one drop-off leaves it empty
Healthy the role is in final selection or trial, or it has 2 or more candidates past matching, or more than 5 fresh people still in matching. Depth always wins, a role in interviews is never marked down for a thin top of funnel
New role opened less than 2 weeks ago, too early to judge, no action needed yet
What the role needs next
In final selectionIn trial the role itself is at that fulfilment status in Airtable. Always counts as Healthy whatever the candidate numbers say, the pipeline already did its job
Needs matching the role is Not healthy or Thin, so the team has to find and put people forward. Always shows on its own, the rest is too low to act on yet
Fresh means the candidate changed status within the last 2 weeks. A stale pool does not count towards health
